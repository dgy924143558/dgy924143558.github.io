Privacy Policy — PackRush

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Overview

PackRush ("the App") is a card pack opening simulator game with original fantasy characters. We respect your privacy.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or registration is required.
  • Your card collection is stored locally on your device only.
  • No usage data or analytics are sent to any server.
  • No third-party SDKs or advertising frameworks are used.
  • The App contains no real-money gambling. Card packs are free and unlimited.
