Privacy Policy — Gojuuon · 五十音閃卡

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Overview

Gojuuon ("the App") is a Japanese kana flashcard application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No personal information is collected.
  • No usage data or analytics are sent to any server.
  • No third-party SDKs or advertising frameworks are used.

Local Storage

Study progress (correct/incorrect counts) is stored locally on your device only and is never shared.

Photo Library

The App requests permission to save the kana chart image to your photo library. This permission is only used when you explicitly tap the save button. No photos are read from your library.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
