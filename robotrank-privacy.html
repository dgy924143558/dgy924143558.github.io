Privacy Policy — RobotRank

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Overview

RobotRank ("the App") lets you browse and rate the world's humanoid robots.

Data Collection

The App does not collect any personal information. No account or registration is required.

Ratings

When you rate a robot, your star rating is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database together with a random anonymous device identifier. This identifier is generated by the App, is not linked to your identity, your Apple ID, or any personal information, and is used only to let you update your own rating. Aggregated scores (averages and vote counts) are visible to all users.

What we never collect

  • No names, emails, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs or advertising frameworks

News Links

The "Latest news" button opens an in-app browser to a public news search. Standard website terms apply while browsing.
